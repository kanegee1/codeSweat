The A1 is a bed-slinger that mostly just works out of the box, but a few habits make a big difference. Here's the checklist I'm keeping for myself.

## Before every print

1. **Wipe the plate.** A little dish soap and warm water beats IPA for removing fingerprint oils on a textured PEI plate.
2. **Check the filament profile.** Generic PLA works, but matching the brand profile in Bambu Studio fixes most stringing.
3. **Run the calibration** after moving the printer or swapping plates.

## Settings worth knowing

| Setting | What I use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layer height | 0.20 mm | Good speed/quality balance |
| Walls | 3 | Stronger functional parts |
| Infill | 15% gyroid | Strong in every direction |
| Brim | Auto | Helps tall, thin parts stick |

> Tip: Give the printer space behind it — the bed travels a lot on the Y axis.
