Secret Lair drops are some of my favorite cards to own, so I want them to stay in great shape. Here's my current system.

## The setup

- **Penny sleeve first.** Every card goes into a soft sleeve before anything else.
- **Ultra Pro toploader.** The sleeved card slides into a rigid toploader to protect against bends.
- **Labeled storage box.** Toploaders stand upright in a box, grouped by drop.

## Cards I actually play

For cards that go into my Commander decks, I double-sleeve instead: an inner perfect-fit sleeve plus a matte outer sleeve. They shuffle well and still stay protected.

## Why bother?

Beyond value, it's about enjoying the art. A clean card in a toploader is easy to pull out and show off.
